          GIFs on cards

          Paste a GIPHY API key to add a Find GIF button to the back of each review card — a picture to remember the word by. The key is kept in this browser only and is never part of an export. Get a free key at developers.giphy.com.

          Translation key

          The free translation endpoint rate-limits after many lookups. Paste a Google Cloud Translation API key and it answers next, before the MyMemory fallback. The free tier is 500,000 characters a month, but the Cloud project needs billing turned on. The key is kept in this browser only and is never part of an export. Create one under Cloud Console → Credentials.

          Korean dictionary

          Paste a krdict Open API key to give Korean words a definition, pronunciation and 초급/중급/고급 level from 한국어기초사전. Without one, Korean words save with their translation only. The key is kept in this browser only and is never part of an export. Get a free key at krdict.korean.go.kr (오픈 API).

          Push to Anki

          Create Anki cards (word → meaning) from your English words, through the AnkiConnect add-on. Open the Anki desktop app first. One-time setup: add this origin to AnkiConnect's webCorsOriginList (Anki → Tools → Add-ons → AnkiConnect → Config), then restart Anki.

          How reviewing works

          The four grades

          Once the answer shows, tell it how well you knew the word.

          Blank
          You didn’t know it. It comes back before this session ends, and its spacing starts over.
          Hard
          You got there, with effort. The next gap grows a little.
          Good
          You knew it. The next gap grows more.
          Easy
          You knew it at once. The gap grows the most.

          If you typed the word, Enter picks the grade for you: Good when it is right (or one of the word’s synonyms), Hard when one or two letters are off, Blank otherwise. Press 1–4 to pick another. A grade can be undone for five seconds.

          When a word comes back

          A word is due as soon as you save it. Each grade then sets its next date, aiming for the point where you would still remember it 9 times in 10. The first time, Blank or Hard brings it back in 1 day, Good in 2 and Easy in 8. Every right answer stretches the gap, more slowly for words you find hard.

          Don’t ask again on a card takes the word out of review. It stays in All words, under Not asked. Ask again on the word brings it back.

          Mastery and word levels

          Mastery is about you. A word is mastered once you have got it right three times in a row and its next review is at least 21 days away. Until then it is learning, and a word you have never graded is new. The Mastery card counts the mastered words in the set you are reviewing.

          A1 to C2 is about the word. It is the word’s CEFR level, from A1 (beginner) to C2 (near-native), and it does not change as you learn. A ~ after it means it was estimated from how common the word is. Korean words use 초급 to 고급, Chinese words HSK 1 to 6.

          Folders made for you, and your own

          Folders made for you fill themselves and cannot be edited: From reading holds every word you save, and Starred the words you star on a card. Your folders are the ones you make, with New folder or by pressing t on a card once the answer shows. A word can be in more than one folder.
