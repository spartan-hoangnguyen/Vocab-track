The long winter

Against every forecast the global supply chains proved unusually resilient through the long winter, despite three separate shocks arriving within a single quarter of one another.

Economists had expected a collapse. Instead inventories held and freight rates normalised within eleven weeks of the first disruption, which surprised almost everybody who had modelled it.

What followed was less a recovery than a quiet reorganisation of how goods move around the world, and it is still going on today.
